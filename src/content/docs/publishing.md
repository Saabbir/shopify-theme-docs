## What's on this page group

- [After Approval](/publishing/after-approval/) — versioning, release notes, and ongoing update rules.

## Quick Reference

- Submission happens through your Partner Dashboard, not a code push.
- Review runs in 5 sequential stages — failing any stage stops the review there.
- Approval isn't the finish line — you're committing to an ongoing update and support cadence.
