The [Theme Store Requirements](/theme-store-requirements/) page has the compliance checklists for performance (Lighthouse scores) and accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA, a set of accessibility standards). That page tells you the pass or fail bar that Shopify's review team checks. This section is different. It shows you the *process* for meeting that bar every time, for every section you build, not just once before you submit.

## What's on this page group

- [Accessibility Deep Dive](/performance-and-accessibility/accessibility-deep-dive/): how to build accessibility into a section from the start, and how to keep it consistent across a whole theme project.
- [Performance Strategy & Critical Rendering Path](/performance-and-accessibility/performance-strategy/): a performance plan, critical CSS, lazy loading, and preload, prefetch, and preconnect.

Media-specific loading and performance (images, video, 3D/AR) moved into its own [Assets Management](/assets/) section, alongside icon management, so everything media-related lives in one place.

## Why this is a process, not just a checklist

The [Accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA)](/theme-store-requirements/accessibility/) and [Performance & Lighthouse](/theme-store-requirements/performance/) pages tell you exactly what Shopify checks, and the lowest score you need to pass. Think of them as a checklist you can scan quickly right before you submit. This section works differently: it helps you build the habit of making good accessibility and performance decisions while you build each section, instead of scrambling to fix 40 sections in the last week before you submit.

## Best practices

- Check every new section against both bars, accessibility and performance, as you build it. A keyboard check and an image-loading check only take a few minutes per section. Fixing a whole theme right before submission takes days.
- Read the deep-dive articles all the way through before you build your first section. Once you understand the reasoning, most of it becomes second nature quickly.
- Before you submit, double-check the [Theme Store Requirements](/theme-store-requirements/) checklists. This section helps you build good habits. That section confirms you actually meet the bar.

## Common mistakes

- **Treating performance and accessibility as something to check only before submission,** instead of a habit for every section. Fixing 40 sections at once is a much bigger job than fixing them one at a time as you go.
- **Reading only the compliance checklist pages and skipping these deep dives.** The checklist tells you the bar. These pages tell you how to clear it every time, without redoing work later.
