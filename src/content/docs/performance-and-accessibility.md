[Theme Store Requirements](/theme-store-requirements/) has the compliance checklists for performance (Lighthouse thresholds) and accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA) — the pass/fail bar Shopify's review team checks. This section is different: it's the *process* for consistently clearing that bar, applied to every section you build, not just verified once before submission.

## What's on this page group

- [Accessibility Deep Dive](/performance-and-accessibility/accessibility-deep-dive/) — building accessibility into a section from the start, and applying it consistently across a whole theme project.
- [Performance Strategy & Critical Rendering Path](/performance-and-accessibility/performance-strategy/) — a performance plan and roadmap, critical CSS, lazy loading, and preload/prefetch/preconnect.
- [Media Optimization: Images, Video & 3D](/performance-and-accessibility/media-optimization/) — loading every media type without paying for it in performance.

## Why "process," not just "checklist"

[Accessibility (WCAG 2.1 AA)](/theme-store-requirements/accessibility/) and [Performance & Lighthouse](/theme-store-requirements/performance/) tell you what Shopify checks and the minimum bar to clear. They're intentionally a checklist — fast to scan before a submission. This section is for building the *habit* that makes clearing that checklist a formality rather than a scramble: a11y and performance decisions made section-by-section, as you build, rather than retrofitted across 40 sections the week before submission.

## Best practices

- Treat every new section against both bars (a11y, performance) as you build it — a keyboard-navigation check and an image-loading check take a few minutes per section; a full-theme retrofit before submission takes days.
- Read the deep-dive articles once, fully, before your first section — most of what they cover becomes a habit quickly, but only if you've seen the reasoning once.
- Cross-reference the [Theme Store Requirements](/theme-store-requirements/) checklists specifically before submission — this section builds the habit, that section verifies the compliance bar.

## Common mistakes

- **Treating performance/accessibility as a pre-submission checklist item** instead of a per-section habit — by the time 40 sections need retrofitting, it's a much bigger job than it would have been section by section.
- **Reading only the compliance checklist pages** and skipping these deep dives — the checklist tells you the bar; these pages tell you how to consistently clear it without rework.
