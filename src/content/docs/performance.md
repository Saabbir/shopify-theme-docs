**TL;DR:** The performance quality bar every section must meet, explained in depth (not just a checklist) — built in from the start, not added later.

The [Performance & Lighthouse](/theme-store-requirements/performance/) page has the compliance checklist: the Lighthouse score bar Shopify's review team checks. That page tells you the pass or fail bar. This section is different. It shows you the *process* for meeting that bar every time, for every section you build, not just once before you submit.

## What's on this page group

- [Performance Strategy & Critical Rendering Path](/performance/performance-strategy/): a performance plan, critical CSS, lazy loading, and preload, prefetch, and preconnect.

Domain-specific performance guidance lives alongside each domain: see [CSS Performance](/css/css-performance/), [JavaScript Performance](/javascript/javascript-performance/), and [Asset Organization & Performance](/assets/asset-organization-and-performance/) for images, video, and 3D/AR media.

## Why this is a process, not just a checklist

The [Performance & Lighthouse](/theme-store-requirements/performance/) page tells you exactly what Shopify checks, and the lowest score you need to pass. Think of it as a checklist you can scan quickly right before you submit. This section works differently: it helps you build the habit of making good performance decisions while you build each section, instead of scrambling to fix 40 sections in the last week before you submit.

## Best practices

- Check every new section against the performance bar as you build it. An image-loading and script-scoping check only takes a few minutes per section. Fixing a whole theme right before submission takes days.
- Read [Performance Strategy](/performance/performance-strategy/) all the way through before you build your first section. Once you understand the reasoning, most of it becomes second nature quickly.
- Before you submit, double-check the [Performance & Lighthouse](/theme-store-requirements/performance/) checklist. This section helps you build good habits. That page confirms you actually meet the bar.

## Common mistakes

- **Treating performance as something to check only before submission,** instead of a habit for every section. Fixing 40 sections at once is a much bigger job than fixing them one at a time as you go.
- **Reading only the compliance checklist page and skipping the deep dive.** The checklist tells you the bar. [Performance Strategy](/performance/performance-strategy/) tells you how to clear it every time, without redoing work later.
